# Support

OP3 Code Sentinel Skill is provided as a community resource.

There is no support guarantee and no obligation to:

- answer issues;
- review pull requests;
- provide fixes;
- maintain compatibility;
- perform security audits;
- investigate incidents;
- provide deployment help;
- provide legal, compliance, or operational advice.

## Best-Effort Community Use

If public issues are enabled, maintainers may respond on a best-effort basis.

Do not include:

- secrets;
- private source code;
- production logs;
- personal data;
- customer data;
- exploit details against third-party systems.

## For Sensitive Use

For production, regulated, high-impact, legal, compliance, or incident-response use, involve qualified humans and follow your organization's procedures.
